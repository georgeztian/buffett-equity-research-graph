# Buffett Investment Analysis

Use this skill when evaluating a company as a potential long-term investment.

## General procedure

1. Understand the company's business model.
2. Evaluate economic moat.
3. Evaluate management quality.
4. Estimate intrinsic value.
5. Evaluate margin of safety.

Do not treat the reference files as company-specific research. They describe Buffett's investment framework.

## Research standards

Need to collect reliable data before any evaluations or judgments. Prefer primary sources:

1. SEC filings
2. Annual reports
3. Investor relations materials
4. Earnings releases

Use reliable secondary sources when primary sources are insufficient.

For important financial numbers, record the source and period.

Clearly distinguish:

- FACT — directly supported by a source
- CALCULATION — derived mathematically from sourced data
- ASSUMPTION — forward-looking assumption
- JUDGMENT — analytical interpretation

Never present an assumption as a historical fact.
