# Margin of Safety

## Buffett's Stated Principle

Only buy when the current market price trades at a discount to your calculated intrinsic value. This buffer protects against analytical errors, unforeseen events, and the fundamental uncertainty of the future.

## Required Discount
- For exceptional businesses (proven wide moats over a long history, excellent management, etc.): Smaller discount (5%-10% below intrinsic value) acceptable
- If company has a long history (10-20 years) of compounding ROE at 20%+: Smaller discount (5%-10%) acceptable
    - Calculate ROE as operating income after taxes divided by book value of equity (do not use net income). Compute operating income after taxes exactly as defined in "Financial data collection" in `references/valuation.md`.
- If very confident in intrinsic value estimate: Smaller discount (5%-10%) acceptable
- If future cash flows are very uncertain or current high growth is likely unsustainable: Larger discount (20%) required
- If durability of economic moats is questionable: Huge discount (50%) required
- If more than one condition above applies, apply the largest required discount.
